# Own-zone Fusion layout

The design follows the selected [own-zone strategy](../../kickoff/selected-strategy.md). Its 680 mm square perimeter leaves 74 mm of perimeter budget. Four swerve modules independently steer to dock at the supply and aim within the alliance zone. The hopper and intake sit above and between the modules. Stowed geometry must be measured again with all mechanisms installed before any inspection claim.

The coordinator's prebuild design uses the first option. The [REV manufacturer reference](https://docs.revrobotics.com/ion-build/motion/maxswerve) and [SDS module reference](https://www.swervedrivespecialties.com/products/mk4i-swerve-module) identify the two alternatives. The 2022 supplier STEP is stored with the owning project locally. Its hash and local axes are in [the layout](layout.json). There is no redistribution grant in the local source packet.

The student archive is authored in Fusion. The portable dimensions and component recipe enable the same student layout to be recreated and independently checked without requiring agents or Fusion during CI. Preserve the original supplier finishes. Public geometry for the drive wheels and modules is an identified packaging representation.
## Open and reproduce R1

Open [the student Fusion archive](own-zone-public.f3d) in Autodesk Fusion. Edit the named part's cross-section sketch or stock-length extrusion. The 18 project components include five hollow structural tubes, five bumper segments and eight drilled module spacers. Each of four public drive representations has a wheel, an envelope for the housing, and a motor reserve. The central front bumper opening is 300 mm. This is a prebuild packaging design. The rail-side spacer attachment, bumper mounting, harness, drivetrain service access under a populated robot and upper mechanisms remain open.

The full local archive is `artifacts/own-zone-native/R1/own-zone-supplier.f3d`. It uses one exact REV supplier definition at four independent, right-handed placements and omits the public proxies. All four reopened with 74 descendant occurrences including the import hierarchy. The wheel centers are at (±268, ±268, 38.1) mm. Each supplier package touches Z = 0 and reaches Z = 171.46 mm. The surrounding perimeter's inner face is at 314.6 mm, leaving 2.15 mm to the extreme 312.45 mm static outer supplier bound. The source depicts one wheel orientation. Full steering sweep and attachment loads still require testing.
