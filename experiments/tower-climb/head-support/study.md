# What holds the lift when power goes away?

At its upper end, the issued 10 mm screw envelope meets a flat, unbored 6 mm plate. At its lower end, the envelope intersects a solid reducer block. Neither feature provides a bearing or a coupling. The parked-geometry study measures both intersections independently. [Foundation check](parked-head.json)

The comparison holds the screw centers at X −240 mm, Y ±305 mm. A 3 mm extension of the upper plate around each screw center backs both faces of a 14 mm thrust-bearing gauge. Each face has 103.67 mm² of annular seat area, with a 3 mm minimum margin to the edge. The gauges follow the dimensions of Kitanihon Seiki EZO F6-14M bearings. Their catalog static rating is 2,440 N. The gauges omit internal bearing details. An engineer still needs to select and retain the radial bush, secure the upper nut, dimension the race pilots, check bearing life and complete the lower drive connection. | Simulated condition | What the result changes |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy symmetric raise | Peak screw reaction is about 386 N per side under a rigid crosshead load-sharing assumption. At 1.5° tilt, the 763 N peak hook load produces modeled screw peaks near 494 and 277 N. |
| Nut blocked under the registered 23 A drive | The hypothetical static thrust reaches 2,862 N at thread friction 0.14 and 5,811 N at 0.035. Either exceeds the catalog static figure. The contact replay does not measure blocked-nut thrust. |
| Drive current restricted to 4.5 A | Two tilted trials stop at about 44.6 and 39.4 mm of the 100 mm stroke. |
| Ideal axial overload trip at 900 N | All six raises reach 100 mm. The low-friction run loses position 3.56 s after power is cut. At the two-second assessment the later loss is still hidden. |
| Two modeled 0.15 N·m fail-closed output brakes | With zero thread friction, a 0.25 s engagement delay and 0.15 s ramp, the shared lift coordinate settles near 97.37 mm with 46.32 mm clearance after twelve unpowered seconds. The half-step test agrees within 0.002 mm. A single 0.15 N·m brake fails this case. |

The first upper-journal variant extends to Z 765 mm, which exceeds the 762 mm starting height. A locally recessed plate drops the bearing seats 7 mm while preserving the mast attachment pads. The spindle top now reaches Z 758 mm and the seated area remains 103.67 mm² per face. The 4 mm nominal height margin still depends on the assembled dimensions, wheel support and floor attitude. [Recessed head geometry](recessed-head-fit.step) · [Recessed head receipt](recessed-head-fit.json)

The retained-head candidate adds an M5 DIN 980V nut and lowers the shaft to Z 754.7 mm. It leaves 1.6 mm of thread above the nut and 2.0 mm from the carriage nut to the lower shoulder when parked. The export contains ten head solids per side. A 275-bound whole-robot check with 0.5 mm added for machining retains 3.725 mm nominal height margin at ±1 mm independent loaded wheel-height variation and 0.649 mm at ±2 mm. The ±3 mm case exceeds the limit by 2.575 mm at an aft tie head. These wheel variations define a sensitivity study. [Retained-head gauge STEP](retained-head-fit.step) · [Whole-height result](retained-head-whole-height.json)

The brake and overload alternatives remain simulations. The shared lift coordinate constrains both sides to move together. A selected assembly also needs independently rated holding devices and a differential-motion test in which one side slips or binds. A one-hook hang has a separate yaw equilibrium problem.
