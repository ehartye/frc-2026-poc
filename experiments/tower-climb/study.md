## What earns points?

On the January 10 V0 and TU00 cutoff, the robot must touch a rung or upright. Other allowed contacts are the tower wall, support structure, FUEL and another robot. G414 forbids full support from an alliance partner. For Level 1 the robot must clear both the carpet and tower base. At Level 2 every bumper must be above the low rung, and at Level 3 above the middle rung. Each robot earns at most one TELEOP level. The three levels yield 10, 20 and 30 points. AUTO Level 1 is worth 15 with up to two robots per alliance. TELEOP evaluation is at rest of all robots or three seconds after cutoff, whichever comes first. During the final 30 seconds G420 protects qualifying contact at the opponent tower. Fouls are referee decisions outside the prescribed rehearsal. At regional/district events the traversal ranking point requires 50 tower points across the entire match. The three project scenarios all finish below 50. Both HUBs stay active during end game. B2/R2 can use the selected lift if their remaining expected FUEL score is smaller than the expected ten-point return after travel, parking and nine seconds of service. B1/R1 serve as hypothetical multi-level comparison peers. ## How it supports the load

The two hooks deploy while the robot is fully supported by its tires. A carriage-mounted horizontal screw seats its captive nut against a rear stop. Then two 2 mm lead vertical screws lower the carriage 100 mm relative to the frame while the low rung holds the hooks. The rear bumper reacts the overturning moment. A nominal gross 62 kg and a loaded 68 kg with its center shifted 35 mm forward are model inputs. Inspection weight excludes bumpers and battery under R103 and needs its own complete build ledger.

At the nominal 11 s assessment each hook carries −244.62 N horizontally and +304.01 N vertically. The upright supplies +489.23 N horizontally, with zero tire reaction. The conservative base clearance is 75.20 mm, including all base-angle tops to 40.00 mm. Using the bottom sheet alone would overstate clearance by 33.00 mm. The tested cutoff is 9 s, followed by a two-second unpowered hold. The nominal screw inputs use 9 mm mean diameter, 6:1 reduction, 0.82 efficiency, 0.14 friction and a 23 A per-motor ceiling. The simulated demand peaks at 2.82 A under the chosen quasi-static screw load. Reflected drive inertia is modeled in the sliding-thread branch. Starting friction, shock, real brake losses, installed travel limits and fixture compliance need build evidence. The longitudinal 4 mm-lead deployment screw shares its carriage with both bearings. Its 20 mm travelling nut retains five nominal turns and the square rails retain at least 87 mm guide overlap. At full rear stroke the nut is seated against its stop, carrying the approximately 489 N terminal axial reaction into the carriage. A 1.24 s smooth unloaded deployment requires about 3,901 rpm at its fastest point. The motor package and an assumed 5,500 rpm no-load speed screen drive feasibility. A failed design had a central rack crossing the upright and two rail tips reaching the rung ahead of the hook crowns. The selected short rails leave that contact to the hooks. ## Reproduce the contact and model check

1. Keep the exact kickoff STEP at an authorized location. Run `python experiments/tower-climb/register.py FIELD.step OUTPUT.json` to reproduce the numeric field registration. The checked-in registration is enough for the remaining steps.
2. Run `python experiments/tower-climb/support.py` for the eight load/control trials and sampled animation clock. Run `python experiments/tower-climb/clearance.py`, `python experiments/tower-climb/field_clearance.py` and `python experiments/tower-climb/extension.py` for the independent solid, field and engagement checks.
3. Open the [Fusion archive](../../cad/tower-lift/tower-l1.f3d) and drive **Tower rear deployment** from 0 to −215 mm and **Tower load stroke** from 0 to −100 mm. Restore both to zero before saving a student copy. The [reopen](../../cad/tower-lift/student-reopen.json) binds the exact native archive, installed occurrences and driven relative transforms. The [native authoring source](../../cad/tower-lift/fusion_climb.py) builds from the original R2C archive and exports the reopened geometry.
4. With OCCT, NumPy and the render dependencies installed, run `python cad/tower-lift/scene.py`, `python scripts/export-showcase-cad.py .` and `python scripts/cad-readmes.py . --template scripts/cad-orbit.html --update`. Use Agent FRC 0.36.0's public-Fusion checker before publishing the site.
