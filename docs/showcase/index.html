The game

Where the FUEL starts

An ordinary match starts with 504 nominal FUEL. Two depots and two outpost chutes hold 24 each. Each robot may preload up to eight. With six full preloads, that leaves 360 nominal pieces in the neutral zone, or 408 with empty robots. Neutral staging can vary by about 24. Recycled pieces return to neutral space.

Our robot can earn one point per FUEL that passes through its own active HUB opening and sensor. An alliance has 110 nominal active seconds in a 160-second match. Both HUBs are active in AUTO, transition and end game. The AUTO result determines which alliance goes inactive first in the four alternating shifts.

Strategic narrative

Stay on the alliance side.

The project coordinator selected the own-zone scorer under delegated authority for this prebuild study. Start AUTO with up to eight supported FUEL. Move into a legal own-zone launch pose. During an inactive shift, collect an allocated batch from the depot or a committed partner. Drive loaded, settle and deliver before the active shift closes.

The tradeoff. The neutral collector can reach more stock, but must clear a trench or cross a bump and share a busy return corridor. In the six-robot AUTO rehearsal, two robots each waited 13.05 s. A taller own-zone design gives up trench access in exchange for storage space. If supply arrives late, score what remains feasible and stage for the next shift.

What would change our choice? A repeatable neutral return that beats own-side supply, or a physical-model test that shows the own-zone buffer jams or misses 2.00 FUEL/s. A missing end wall in the intake study allowed loose stock to leave the field. Collection under field constraints, loaded driving and repeated scoring remain open.
